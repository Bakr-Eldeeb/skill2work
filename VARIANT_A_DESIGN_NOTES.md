# Visual and interaction decisions

The passport cover sits on a violet and mint field beside a sample evidence slip. Only the public page uses the fictional Nour Adel example. Job details continue to obtain scores from the matching module. Unassessed rows show their evidence state and employer minimum without a filled score.

The journey uses native links. The sample evidence control supports Enter and Space. Native disclosure controls expose the job comparison and contribution calculation. The four stages remain in document order on narrow screens. Logical CSS properties place the score marker and timeline rail correctly in Arabic.

Motion is brief and optional. Reduced-motion preferences suppress entrances and bar fills. The ticket retains its QR quiet zone and existing download and print paths. University coverage labels use the existing ratio of assessed students to requesting companies, as explained in the analytics screen.

Run locally with Node 24, npm ci and npm start. Demo credentials are in DEMO_ACCOUNTS.md.
