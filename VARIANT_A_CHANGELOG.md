# Immersive Career Journey

- The public hero reuses the actual passport cover. A keyboard-operated preview reveals a fictional candidate’s assessment and a related role. Four career stages link into the candidate workspace through the existing role guard.
- Candidate, company and university panels show examples of evidence, minimum requirements and fair registration.
- Job details add a collapsible Skill X-Ray comparison. The filled bar shows assessed evidence and the vertical marker shows the employer’s minimum.
- Fair tickets have a perforated divider and entrance motion. Project technologies appear as tags, career entries have a timeline rail, and current application milestones have a highlighted node.
- University analytics animate existing demand and supply bars. Empty states select artwork for tickets, skills, hiring, approvals or search.
- Pages CI runs the source checks before packaging. The two landing headline assertions now reflect the new copy.
